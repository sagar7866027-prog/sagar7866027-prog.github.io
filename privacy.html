Privacy Policy

App: QUIZ MASTER
Developer: Sagar Dey Sarkar
Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. Summary

This app does not collect your name, email, phone number, location, photos or contacts. There are no accounts or logins in the app. We do not sell your personal data or share it with anyone. The app shows ads (from Google AdMob or Unity Ads) only to users aged 18 or older (see section 5).

2. Data stored on your device

To make the app work, some data is saved only inside your phone: your chosen language, sound on/off, level and progress, the lists of cleared and seen questions, bonus-lifeline status, an ad-display counter, whether you accepted the Terms and Privacy Policy, and an advertising age category. On the age screen, your date of birth is used only on the device to calculate your age; the date of birth itself is not saved. Only the broad category (under 18 / 18 or older) is stored locally. This data is not sent to any server of ours. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Internet use

The app can be played without internet. The internet is used to show ads to users aged 18 or older.

In the direct-download version of the app, when the app opens it checks for a new version by reading a small file (version.json) hosted on GitHub. The app does not send any personal information in this request. However, as with any internet request, GitHub can see your IP address and standard request details, which it handles under its own policy (GitHub Privacy Statement). If a new version exists, you may choose to download it; the server hosting the download link can likewise see your IP address. App-store versions do not perform this check.

4. Text-to-Speech

To read questions and answers aloud, the app uses your phone's Text-to-Speech engine (usually Google Text-to-Speech). That is a service on your device; the engine may use the internet to download voice data. It is governed by its own provider's policy.

5. Ads and third parties (Google AdMob and Unity Ads)

We do not ourselves collect or sell your personal data. However, the app shows ads from Google AdMob only to users aged 18 or older; no ads are shown to users under 18, and AdMob is not even started for them. While serving ads, Google and its partners may use some device information (such as IP address, device type and operating system, advertising ID or other device identifiers, and information about ad interactions and errors) to deliver ads, limit ad frequency, prevent fraud and measure ad performance. Personalized ads are turned off in this app and only ads suitable for all audiences (rating G) are requested. Your date of birth is not sent to Google.

These ads include optional rewarded ads: users aged 18 or older may choose to watch one to get an extra in-app lifeline. Watching is never required; we take no extra personal data for it, and the data use is the same as for other AdMob ads.

Which ad network runs depends on where you installed the app. If it is installed from the Google Play Store, Samsung Galaxy Store, Xiaomi GetApps, OPPO App Market, VIVO App Store or Amazon Appstore, ads come from Google AdMob. If it is installed from any other store (for example Indus Appstore) or from a direct APK file, ads come from Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). Only one of these two networks runs on a given phone. The same basic rules apply to both: no ads and no ad network for users under 18, and no date of birth is sent. For Unity Ads, the kind of ads and the use of data follow Unity's own policies and settings. Unity and its partners may likewise use device information such as IP address, device type and operating system, advertising ID and ad interaction data to deliver ads, limit frequency, prevent fraud and measure performance. Unity's policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Google's policies (AdMob): policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Children and age

This app is a general-knowledge quiz suitable for all ages. On first use, a neutral age screen asks the user to select their date of birth. The date of birth is calculated on the device and is neither saved nor sent to us; only the broad age category (under 18 / 18 or older) is stored locally. No ads are shown to users under 18. For users aged 18 or older, ads are shown with the maximum content rating set to G and personalized ads disabled. For users under 18, changing the date of birth requires solving a parental-gate sum. Parents or guardians with questions are welcome to contact us at the email below.

7. App stores

This app may be available through more than one app store. The store you install it from may collect its own information about installs, updates and crashes under its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

8. Your rights and deleting data

We do not keep any of your data on our side, so there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. Even so, you may send any question or request about the rights you have under applicable law (such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) to the email below. To remove the progress saved on your phone, clear the app's data in Settings > Apps > QUIZ MASTER or uninstall the app. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads.

9. Security

Your data stays on your phone and is not stored on any server of ours. To protect the app's content, screenshots and screen recording are disabled.

10. Changes

This policy may change from time to time. When it does, the "Last updated" date above will change and you will be asked to accept it again in the app.
