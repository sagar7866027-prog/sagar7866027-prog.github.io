QUIZ MASTER

General-knowledge quiz app · Bangla / English / Hindi

QUIZ MASTER is a fun general-knowledge quiz for Android. Test yourself with questions in Bangla, English and Hindi, move up through levels, and use lifelines when a question gets tricky.

Features

  • Three age groups: primary, under 18, and 18+, each with its own questions
  • 50 levels per group, 10 questions per level
  • Lifelines: Skip and 50/50
  • Questions can be read aloud (Text-to-Speech)
  • Works offline; no account or login needed
  • No ads for users under 18

Privacy
